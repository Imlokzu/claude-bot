# Asset sources

Everything shipped in `public/` is either public domain or CC-licensed for
commercial use. Links are here out of respect for the authors — where the
licence requires attribution, that is noted.

## 3D models

- **Asteroid shape models** — `public/models/asteroids/*.stl`
  [NASA 3D Resources](https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources), public domain.
  Real radar and flyby-derived shape models: 216 Kleopatra, 4179 Toutatis,
  6489 Golevka, 1620 Geographos, 4486 Mithra, 8567 1996 HW1.
- **Boulders** — `public/models/rocks/*`
  [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/models), CC0.
  Photogrammetry scans: `boulder_01`, `rock_09`, `namaqualand_boulder_03`.
- **CRT monitor** — `public/models/crt.glb`
  "CRT Monitor" by **Jarlan Perez** via [Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza/m/8jVB0zIXKCv),
  CC-BY 3.0 — *attribution required*.

## Textures

- **Ice / rock PBR maps** — `public/textures/ice-*.webp`
  [Ice004](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Ice004), ambientCG, CC0.
  Downscaled to 1K and converted to WebP for the web.

## Brand marks

Model logos are drawn from vector paths at runtime, not bundled as files:

- Most come from [simple-icons](https://simpleicons.org), CC0.
- OpenAI, Grok and Z.ai/GLM marks are taken from each company's own logo
  files via Wikimedia Commons. These are trademarks of their owners and are
  used here to identify the services the bot connects to.

## Mascot

The pixel crab is ported from this project's own Virtual Bot
(`Virtual Bot/static/crab.js`), itself derived from
[PixelClaw](https://github.com/masasron/PixelClaw) by Ron Masas, MIT.
